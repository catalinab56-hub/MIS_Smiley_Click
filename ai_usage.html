Correct AI Usage

AI should be used to help with tasks, not to complete an entire task. AI can also be used to edit writing grammatically without changing the meaning of your writing or adding to it. Additionally, AI can be used to help simplify topics that people may not understand, helping to word them in a better way for someone to understand the information they are trying to find. Lastly, AI can also be used as a simulator to prepare for interviews or to help you get ready for various scenarios.

AI works best as a helper for individual tasks rather than a replacement for doing the whole task yourself. It can also proofread your writing for grammar and mechanics without changing your meaning or adding ideas of its own. Additionally, it can explain confusing topics in simpler terms, making it easier to understand the information you’re looking for. Lastly, AI can act as a practice partner, letting you rehearse for interviews or run through other situations before they happen.

Benefits of AI

  1. Assists with tasks without replacing human effort.

  2. Acts as a simulator for interview preparation and scenario practice.

Who Benefits?

  1. Companies looking to enhance efficiency and decision-making.

  2. Educators and students for learning and teaching purposes.

How AI Tools Used

  • For proofreading and editing writing.

  • To explain confusing topics in simpler terms.

AI has ways it can be used right and ways it can be used wrong. We must be mindful of how we utilize AI to ensure it benefits us without causing harm or violating ethical standards.

Incorrect AI Usage

Incorrect AI Usage Examples

  • Academic dishonesty: Using AI to complete assignments or exams without proper authorization.

  • Privacy violations: Using AI to access or manipulate personal data without consent.

  • Over-reliance: Relying on AI for critical decisions without human oversight.

AI usage is incorrect if you copy and paste the generated information and present it as your own without putting any valid effort into the assignment. Using AI without the approval of a professor while taking closed-book exams, quizzes, and homework is an incorrect use of AI, as it violates academic integrity in a school setting. Additionally, using AI to harm someone’s identity or using it as a form of defamation in any way is also an incorrect use of AI. Overall, AI should not be used to harm others or as a way to finish assignments without putting in effort or without proper permission from a professor.

Using AI is wrong when you copy and paste its output and present it as your own work without making a real effort on the assignment. It’s also wrong to use AI during closed-book exams, quizzes, or homework without your professor’s approval, because doing so breaks academic integrity rules. Using AI to damage someone’s reputation or to defame them in any way is likewise a misuse. In short, AI should never be used to hurt others, and it shouldn’t be used to skip the effort an assignment requires or to bypass a professor’s permission.
