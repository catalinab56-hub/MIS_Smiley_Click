Malware and Cyber Threats

The ILOVEYOU Worm

Released on May 4, 2000 the ILOVEYOU worm, also known as the Love Bug, is a computer worm that spread via email in 2000. It used a subject line of "ILOVEYOU" and an attachment named "LOVE-LETTER-FOR-YOU.TXT.vbs" to trick users into opening it. Once opened, the worm would overwrite files on the victim's computer and send itself to everyone in the victim's email contacts, causing widespread damage and disruption which caused damages estimated to be between $3 billion to as much as $15 billion. This lead to increased public awareness and reassessment of cybersecurity practices, leading to changes in how antivirus companies distributed updates due to the rapid spread and impact of the worm.
