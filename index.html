Management information systems (MIS), artificial intelligence (AI), and data analytics are all important aspects that help companies grow effectively, but integrating them to work together can allow companies to plan incredibly well for future business growth. MIS collects raw data from different areas of a corporation, including sales, inventory, and human resources, to grow and improve a company’s business strategy. Therefore, by applying data analytics in MIS, companies can structure this data to run reports on performance across different areas of the corporation to compare historical business performance trends. This data input into this system is limited to simple charting in order to compare performance, but introducing AI allows for more automatic predictive modeling. Instead of individual employees having to take time to analyze multiple spreadsheets of past data for months before a coherent view, AI is capable of learning and scanning all of these spreadsheets to instantly provide useful feedback to the company. Artificial intelligence can automate these longer tasks of analyzing large amounts of data, which can help free up employees to focus on other company aspects involving creative problem-solving and other task execution for company growth.

Management information systems (MIS), artificial intelligence (AI), and data analytics each help companies grow, but integrating them lets a company plan for future growth far more effectively. MIS collects raw data from across a corporation, including sales, inventory, and human resources, and uses it to improve the company’s business strategy. When data analytics is applied within an MIS, the company can organize that data into performance reports and compare trends in its historical results. On its own, this process is limited to simple charts and comparisons, but adding AI makes automated predictive modeling possible. Instead of employees spending months working through spreadsheets of past data, AI can scan and learn from all of them at once and quickly give the company useful feedback. Because AI automates this time-consuming analysis, employees are free to focus on creative problem-solving and on carrying out the work that drives company growth.
